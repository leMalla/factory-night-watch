Version: 0.3.5
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Select circuit networks through any connected circuit entity, including chests and inserters.
    - Deduplicate the same network in area selections and ignore copper power connections.
    - Require explicit selection when red/green or input/output networks are ambiguous.
    - Store the exact connector; keep input/output values separate across save/load.
  Compatibility:
    - Existing pole-based rules retain their original red/green connector.
    - Source coordinates remain available for alarm-location viewing.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Native bottom-right shortcut and three-tab control panel.
    - Named circuit conditions with signal, quality, red/green network, comparison and continuous delay.
    - Missing circuit sources trigger a latched safety pause while offline.
    - Complete German and English runtime messages and controls.

    - Explicit label widths, save/discard drafts, dirty-close and test confirmation.
    - Retain tab/window position; read-only controls for non-admins.
